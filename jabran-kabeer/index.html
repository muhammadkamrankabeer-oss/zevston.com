Profile

Interiors planned around how a space is actually used.

Muhammad Jabran Kabeer is a co-founder of Zevston, based in Lahore, Pakistan. He leads the interiors division, covering space planning, décor, custom furniture and in-house woodwork.

Kitchens, wardrobes and office interiors are designed around storage, workflow and finish quality, then built with in-house woodwork to the exact dimensions of the space.

At Zevston he works alongside his brother, civil engineer Muhammad Imran Kabeer, so the interior is planned together with the structure.
