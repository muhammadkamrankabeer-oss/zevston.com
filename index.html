About Zevston

A practical partner for your next project.

Zevston is a Lahore-based company built around two complementary divisions: civil engineering and construction, and interior design with custom woodwork. The two work together so a project can move from foundation to final finish without switching contractors.

Our goal is straightforward: understand the requirement, plan the work carefully, supervise execution and deliver a finished project — structure and interior — that meets the agreed expectations.
