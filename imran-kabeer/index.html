Profile

Civil engineering built on site experience.

Muhammad Imran Kabeer is a co-founder of Zevston and a civil engineer based in Lahore, Pakistan, with 13 years of hands-on experience in the country's construction industry. He leads the construction division at Zevston.

Muhammad Imran Kabeer's work covers project planning, site supervision, structural coordination and cost management, from residential houses to large-scale commercial projects.

At Zevston he works alongside his brother and co-founder Muhammad Jabran Kabeer, who leads interiors, woodwork and décor, so a project can move from foundation to final finish with one team.
